Senior capstone · FPGA · Computer vision

FPGA stereo-vision tennis ball tracker

A real-time pipeline for finding a moving ball in stereo images and reconstructing its position in three-dimensional space. The system joined VHDL image processing, camera integration, physics-based simulation, and formal design reviews into one testable architecture.

  • Filtered and thresholded stereo images on a Snickerdoodle FPGA.
  • Detected image centroids, calculated disparity, and reconstructed 3D position.
  • Modeled stereo geometry in MATLAB and built a Blender test environment.
  • Integrated Python camera and control logic with Simulink communication over Wi-Fi.
